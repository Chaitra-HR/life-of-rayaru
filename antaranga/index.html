01 — PŪRVĀŚRAMA

VENKATANATHA. SCHOLAR. TEACHER. GṚHASTHA.

Before accepting Sannyasa, Rayaru was known as Venkatanatha. He was born into a family of scholars and musicians and was trained in grammar, logic, Mimamsa and Vedanta.

He studied under Lakshminarasimhacharya and later Sri Sudhindra Tirtha. His ability in Vyakarana earned him the title Mahabhashyacharya.

Venkatanatha lived through severe poverty, but continued his adhyayana and adhyapana. He taught students without treating knowledge as a means of personal gain.

During Venkatanatha’s Aksharabhyasa, Thimmanna Bhatta wrote Om and explained that it represented the Supreme Lord.

HOW COULD SO SMALL A SYMBOL EXPRESS THE INFINITE GREATNESS OF LORD HARI?

When his father asked him to explain, the young Venkatanatha described the eight components of Pranava:

A·U·M·Nada·Bindu·Ghosha·Shanta·Atishanta

He related them to eight forms of the Supreme and continued through the Vyahritis, the Vedas and Purusha Sukta, explaining Om through the infinite auspicious attributes of Lord Hari.

The assembled scholars were astonished by the depth of the child’s explanation.

Following his father’s passing, Venkatanatha continued his education under Lakshminarasimhacharya at Madurai.

Venkatanatha married Saraswati Bai. Their son, Lakshminarayana, was born during his grihasthashrama.
